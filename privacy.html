Privacy

How we look after your information.

This explains what information ICE keeps about members, families and carers, why we need it, who can see it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who we are

ICE – Inclusive Community Experiences runs social and community activities for young people aged 16 and over with additional needs in Weston-super-Mare, Portishead and Clevedon.

ICE is responsible for the information in this policy (the “data controller”). This covers our website, the ICE app for iPhone, and the information you give us in person, by email or by phone.

Questions about your information: hello.ice@icloud.com

The short version

  • We only collect what we need to run safe, enjoyable sessions.
  • A member's support and health information is seen only by the people who look after them and the ICE team.
  • We never sell your information, show adverts, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • You can see, correct or delete your information, and delete your app account at any time from the app.

What we collect

WhatWhy we need it
Your account: name, email address, and phone number if you give itTo sign you in, and so ICE can contact you about bookings.
About each member: name, date of birth, town, and what they enjoyTo know who is coming and plan sessions they'll enjoy.
Support and health: support needs, how they communicate, worries and what helps, allergies, medication and medical conditions, food and drinkSo staff can keep members safe and support them properly at every session.
Emergency contacts, and for members under 18, a parent or carer's name and contact detailsTo reach the right person if something happens, and so a parent or carer can approve the pack.
A photo of each memberSo staff recognise members when they arrive.
Consents: photos on ICE social media, sharing needs with session staff, travelling home aloneSo we follow your wishes.
Documents you choose to add, such as a care planTo help staff support a member.
Bookings, attendance and whether a session has been paid forTo run sessions and keep track of places and payments.
Messages between you and ICETo answer your questions and keep you updated.
Website enquiries: your name, contact details, town and what you tell usTo reply when you ask to join.
ICE staff: first name and photoSo families know who they're talking to.

The app also keeps a few settings on your phone, such as whether you've sent an enquiry. It doesn't use your location, contacts or calendar unless you choose to add a session to your calendar or ICE to your contacts, and then only on your phone.

We don't take payments in the app. If you pay through a Monzo payment link, Monzo handles that payment under its own privacy policy.

Our legal reasons (UK GDPR)

  • Running the service you've asked for (contract, or our legitimate interests in running safe activities): accounts, bookings, messages, contact and emergency details.
  • Health and support information is “special category” data. We use it with your explicit consent, given when you send the member pack, and where needed to protect someone's vital interests in an emergency.
  • Photos on social media only with consent, which you can change at any time in the app.

Children and young people

ICE is for young people aged 16 and over. If a member under 18 fills in their own member pack, ICE contacts their parent or carer for approval before they can book. Parents and carers can look after a member's information in the app on their behalf.

Who can see it

  • The ICE team, to run sessions and support members.
  • The people who look after a member – parents, carers or support workers ICE has linked to them – and the member themselves if they have their own account. Families never see other families' information.
  • Other members going to the same session may see your first name only, if ICE chooses to show who's going.

We use these companies to run the app. They only handle your information on our instructions:

  • Supabase stores the app's information, photos and documents on servers in London.
  • Resend sends sign-in codes and emails from ICE.
  • Apple delivers notifications to your iPhone. Notifications on the lock screen never show message text or health details.
  • Cloudflare runs our website and email domain.

We don't share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it, or we need to in an emergency or to keep someone safe.

How long we keep it

  • While someone is an ICE member, we keep their information so we can support them at sessions.
  • If you delete your app account, your sign-in, name and messages with ICE are deleted straight away.
  • When someone leaves, we keep their member record, bookings and payment records only as long as we need them, for example for safeguarding or our accounts, and then delete them. Ask us if you'd like to know more.
  • Website enquiries we don't go ahead with are deleted when we no longer need them.

Keeping it safe

Information is encrypted when it travels between your phone and our servers. Every family can only reach the members they look after, and only the ICE team can see everyone. Photos and documents are kept in private storage, not on public web links. Signing in uses a one-time code instead of a password.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you or a member you look after;
  • correct anything that's wrong (you can also edit most of it yourself in the app);
  • delete your information;
  • stop using it, or limit how we use it;
  • withdraw a consent at any time.

Deleting your account: in the app, go to Account and tap Delete account. You can also ask ICE to delete the records of members only you look after. Or email us and we'll do it for you.

To use any of these rights, email hello.ice@icloud.com. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your information, we'll tell you in the app or by email.
